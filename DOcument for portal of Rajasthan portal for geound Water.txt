IntroductionGroundwater is a critical natural resource that supports drinking water supply, agriculture, industrial growth, and infrastructure development. With increasing pressure on groundwater due to urbanization, industrial expansion, and climate variability, there is a growing need for transparent, scientific, and technology-driven groundwater governance.At present, groundwater-related services such as No Objection Certificate (NOC) issuance, rig registration, groundwater abstraction monitoring, and compliance enforcement are largely handled through form-based digital systems or semi-manual processes. While these systems have enabled online submission, they continue to face challenges such as incomplete applications, repetitive document deficiencies, prolonged processing time, heavy dependency on manual scrutiny, and limited analytical or decision-support capability.To address these challenges, it is proposed to design and develop a comprehensive Groundwater Website and Online Governance Portal that integrates public information dissemination, online service delivery, scientific validation, and intelligent decision support into a single unified digital platform.The proposed system is not limited to digitizing existing forms or workflows. Instead, it aims to establish a full-scale digital groundwater ecosystem consisting of:A public-facing website for awareness, transparency, and data accessA secure transactional portal for applicants, rig operators, officers, and administratorsA GIS-based spatial intelligence layer for groundwater validationAn AI-assisted compliance and guidance layer to improve application quality and processing efficiencyThe platform will support all major groundwater-related services, including:Groundwater abstraction NOC for Industries, Mining, and Infrastructure projectsRig registration and drilling permit managementRenewal and compliance reportingCharges, penalties, and environmental compensation calculationGroundwater information systems and public data accessBy combining user-centric design, scientific groundwater data, GIS validation, and AI-based compliance assistance, the system will significantly reduce processing delays, improve data quality, minimize rejections due to procedural errors, and enhance overall governance efficiency.OBJECTIVES OF THE SYSTEMThe primary objective of the proposed Groundwater Website and Online Governance Portal is to establish a comprehensive, transparent, and technology-enabled system for effective management, regulation, and sustainable utilization of groundwater resources.The specific objectives of the system are outlined below.2.1
